ANDROID MAPPING SUGGESTION
--------------------------
- Each hash route = Activity or NavController destination
  Example: hod/task-detail?id=room-305 → TaskDetailFragment
- Bottom nav = BottomNavigationView (4–5 items per role)
- data-nav attributes = intent destinations in prototype
